Who we are

Students making the case for civic education.

Our Education side teaches civics to 4th–8th graders. YCN NJ Policy works on the bigger picture. We research how New Jersey's schools and towns handle civic education, and we advocate for bills and local policies that give every student a stronger foundation.

Like everything at YCN, it's planned and run entirely by students.

What we do

Research. Teach. Advocate.

We work at the state and local level to make civic education stronger across New Jersey.

01

Research

We dig into New Jersey bills, budgets and school policies using primary sources like the Legislature, state agencies and public data.

02

Teach

We turn what we learn into lessons and plain-language explainers, so students and families understand how these decisions get made.

03

Advocate

We support state and local bills that expand and strengthen civic education, and we make the case for them as students.

04

Partner

We work directly with school boards and town councils to bring more civic education into their schools and communities.

Our promise

One cause. No sides.

Our only cause is civic education.

We advocate only for policies that expand and strengthen civic education. That's the whole agenda.

No parties. No candidates.

We never endorse or oppose any political party or candidate, and we'll work with anyone who wants students to understand their government.

Our classrooms stay neutral.

Advocacy happens at board meetings and in Trenton, not in our lessons. What we teach kids is checked by outside observers for neutrality.
