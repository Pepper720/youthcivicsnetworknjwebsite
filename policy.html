Who we are

Understanding policy shouldn't take a law degree.

YCN NJ Policy is the newest part of Youth Civics Network New Jersey. Our Education side teaches civics to 4th–8th graders. The Policy team is for high schoolers who want to go further and understand how decisions actually get made in Trenton and in towns across the state.

Like everything at YCN, it's planned and run entirely by students.

What we do

Research it. Explain it. Teach it.

The policy team turns real New Jersey issues into work that helps people understand them.

01

Research

We dig into New Jersey bills, budgets and issues using primary sources like the Legislature, state agencies and public data.

02

Explain

We turn what we learn into plain-language explainers that cover what a policy does, who it affects, and what each side argues.

03

Teach

Our research helps shape future YCN NJ lessons, so the civics we teach keeps up with what's happening in New Jersey.

Our promise

Nonpartisan, always.

We explain. We don't endorse.

We never take positions on bills, candidates or parties. Our job is to help people understand an issue, not to tell them what to think.

Every side, fairly.

When people disagree, we lay out the main arguments on each side in their strongest form, with sources you can check.

The same standard as our lessons.

Outside observers proofread every YCN lesson for neutrality. Our policy work is held to that same standard.
