Who we are

Bringing civic engagement to every community.

Youth Civics Network NJ is the New Jersey branch of the national Youth Civics Network. Our mission is to make civics nonpartisan, digestible, and genuinely enjoyable for elementary and middle schoolers.

Everything we do is planned, designed, built and taught by high schoolers. Students run every part of it, from statewide planning down to each local chapter.

    Every lesson mixes a warm-up, a short lesson, and a game or hands-on activity, and outside reviewers check each one to make sure it stays nonpartisan. Take a look at the real slides.

    Module 1 Sharing Power

    How NJ and the Federal Government Share Power

    What’s inside
    • Why the U.S. Constitution works like a Little League rulebook
    • Which powers are federal, shared, or New Jersey's own
    • The Supremacy Clause and checks and balances

    ActivityTurn a federal rule into a New Jersey law

    Module 2 State Politics

    New Jersey State Politics

    What’s inside
    • The Governor's powers and three kinds of veto
    • 120 lawmakers, 7 justices, and how they work together
    • Statewide issues: school funding, transit, and the Jersey Shore

    ActivityEach group is a NJ town that has to solve a problem

    Module 3 Local Politics

    Local Politics in New Jersey

    What’s inside
    • 21 counties, 564 municipalities, and Home Rule
    • Mayors, town councils, and school boards
