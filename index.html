Who we are

Bringing civic engagement to every community.

Youth Civics Network NJ is the New Jersey branch of the national Youth Civics Network. Our mission is to make civics nonpartisan, digestible, and genuinely enjoyable for elementary and middle schoolers.

Everything we do is planned, designed, built and taught by high schoolers. Students run every part of it, from statewide planning down to each local chapter.
