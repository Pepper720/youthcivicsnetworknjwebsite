Who we are

Bringing civic engagement to every classroom.

Youth Civics Network NJ is the New Jersey branch of the national Youth Civics Network. Our mission is to make civics nonpartisan, digestible, and genuinely enjoyable for elementary and middle schoolers.

Everything we do is planned, designed, built and taught by high schoolers. Students run every part of it, from the state board down to each local chapter.

Our curriculum

Big ideas, made simple.

Every lesson mixes a warm-up, a short lesson, and a game or hands-on activity.

1

How NJ and the Federal Government Share Power

Think of it like a Little League rulebook. Students learn who makes which rules, then write a state law of their own.

2

New Jersey State Politics

The three branches, what the Governor can (and can't) do, and a team challenge to solve a real town's problem.

3

Local Politics

NJ has 21 counties and 564 municipalities. Students meet school boards and learn how local decisions shape their everyday life.

4

How Elections and Voting Work

Registration, the Electoral College, a Blooket quiz, and a "pizza or pasta" mini Electoral College showdown.
